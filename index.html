About

// who's behind the console

I work in IT and endpoint security at a cloud-native startup, managing a mixed Windows/macOS/mobile fleet across Intune, Jamf, Entra ID, and Microsoft Defender. My day-to-day is device hardening, identity and access, and improving security posture.

This portfolio is where I take that further — building networks from scratch to understand how traffic really moves, where it breaks, and how an analyst detects and contains an intrusion. I learn by building the thing, breaking the thing, and writing down exactly what happened.

Targeting security analyst and cyber systems roles where that instinct to instrument, document, and defend is the job.
